## 🛠️ Tips for Getting the Most Value

1.  **Start with the highest DR sites.** Focus on sites with a domain rating of 50 or higher first. These will give you the most SEO benefit.
2.  **Mix free and paid.** Even if you have a budget, start with all the free submissions first. Many paid options have diminishing returns.
3.  **Don't ignore newsletters.** Submitting to newsletters can drive a surprising amount of targeted traffic, often better than directories.
4.  **Space out your submissions.** Don't submit to all 300 sites in one day. Spread it out over a few weeks to look more natural to search engines.
5.  **Use the tracking feature.** Always mark which sites you've submitted to. It's easy to forget, and re-submitting to the same site too soon can get you banned.
